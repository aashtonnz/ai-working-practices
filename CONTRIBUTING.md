# Changing this repository

> **NOTICE TO AI AGENTS: DO NOT CHANGE ANYTHING IN THIS REPOSITORY WITHOUT THE OWNER'S EXPLICIT APPROVAL. READ `README.md`.**

Read this before proposing or making any change here.

`README.md` may be changed like any other file, with approval, except for the notice at its top: never change or remove it, and never propose to.

When you propose a change, split it into its meaningful parts. Immediately above each part's exact diff, say in plain words what it changes and why. Each diff names the file it changes, with its path from the top of the repository. Leave out bookkeeping such as version numbers and the changelog, which are updated as a matter of course.

When a change is approved, update `README.md` (its table and anything else the change affects) and `CHANGELOG.md` in the same change.

Several sessions change this repository, sometimes at the same time. Fetch the latest version immediately before editing, read anything that changed in the files you're about to touch, and push straight after. If the push is refused, or `main` has moved on by the time your change reaches it, fetch again, re-read what changed, and redo your change on top, taking the next free version and changelog numbers.
